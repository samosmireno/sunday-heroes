================================================================
RULES · paste once before the first prompt
================================================================

These rules hold for everything you draw from now on in this project.

- Follow the design system and the screens already in this project. Reuse
  their panels, rows, tags, stickers, sheets and shells; invent no new
  component where one exists.
- Draw every section at 390 px (phone shell) beside 1440 px (desktop
  shell), labelled like the existing sections (18a, 18b ...).
- Shadows are black, offset down and left: -4px 4px on a phone, -8px 8px
  on a desktop. Left edge tab on every panel. 1 px ink stroke on shapes.
- A competition's title band is paper with the name in ink; only the
  Format tag carries the family colour (Pickup #3a3ab0, League #0c542c,
  Knockout #f93a07).
- An offset title is panel blue #3a3ab0 text over a black copy set down
  and left. Never ink text with a blue shadow.
- Button labels are plain, never underlined. The paper circle appears only
  on a button that adds something, with a + inside it. No other button
  has a circle.
- Never skew or rotate text; tilt only the shape behind it. The one
  exception is the sticker card's corner ribbon, whose text runs along
  the ribbon.
- Every tap target is 44 px. List and picker rows never shrink
  (flex: 0 0 48px). No label below 12 px.
- Never truncate a name with an ellipsis: widen the cell, wrap, or shrink
  the numbers.
- Never set text straight on grass; put it on a paper strip in ink. Text
  on the light green is ink.
- Dark swatches (Navy, Black, Dark green) get a paper stroke on navy rows.
- On a phone a dialog is a full-screen sheet with a title, Cancel, a
  scrolling body and a sticky action bar. On a desktop it is a dialog or
  a menu.
- Dates read "14 Sep", or "12 Oct 2025" outside the current year. Never
  14.09.2026.
- A win rate is written "67% · 8-2-2". A rating is written "2.10 · 18".
- Paging is one paper "Show more" bar under a list, and only where a
  prompt says so. No page numbers, no totals, no infinite scroll.
- Warnings are yellow inline stickers with ink text; refusals are red.
- Never use these words: Dashboard, Home or Away for a side, Pending
  Votes, Registered, Unregistered, Duel, Leaderboard, Draft, subs,
  Matchday, walkover for a bye.

5 · Overflow menus on a Fixture and on a Tie
================================================================

Draw the "⋯" overflow opened from one Fixture row of the League Schedule
(screen 12) and from one Tie card of the Knockout Bracket (screen 13).
The "⋯" button is 44 px and keeps its three dots visible while active.

Phone: a bottom sheet titled with the pairing ("Bulls v Plavi · Round 8").
Desktop: a menu anchored to the button. Each item is a 48 px row: a label
and, where useful, one muted line beneath. An item that cannot be used is
greyed with its reason as the muted line. A destructive item is in brand
red and sits last, under a divider. Items only an admin sees sit under a
small heading "Admin".

Draw these sections, as seen by a Manager unless said otherwise:
  a. a Fixture to play: Set date · Move to another Round · Swap the order
     of the sides · Walkover ... · Mark Not played
  b. a Fixture marked Not played: only Restore is live, the rest greyed
  c. a Fixture that is a Walkover: only Undo walkover is live
  d. an Extra Fixture to play: as (a), plus Delete in red
  e. a Completed Fixture: Open match · Change record, nothing else
  f. a Tie to play, seen by an admin: Set date · Swap the order of the
     sides · Walkover ... · Mark Not played, and under "Admin": Withdraw
     a team ...
  g. a bye line in the bracket, seen by an admin: one item under "Admin",
     Add a team ...
  h. any of them seen by a Member: no "⋯" button at all

Then the Walkover sheet that "Walkover ..." opens: title "Walkover", the
two Teams as two large selectable plates (colour and name), the sentence
"The match is awarded 3-0. Nobody gets a lineup, goals or a vote.", and a
sticky bar with Cancel and the red "Award to Bulls", which is disabled
until a plate is picked.
